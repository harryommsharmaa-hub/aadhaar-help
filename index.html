Suspended or Deactivated Aadhaar

Important information and safe steps to seek official assistance.

What does it mean?

If you are told that your Aadhaar is suspended, deactivated or not usable, it is important to confirm the exact status and reason directly with UIDAI. Different error messages can have different meanings. Do not assume that every authentication failure means Aadhaar has been deactivated.

Suspended vs. Deactivated

People may use these terms differently. The exact status and applicable process must be confirmed by UIDAI. An authentication failure alone does not prove that an Aadhaar number is suspended or deactivated.

Possible reasons to ask UIDAI about

  • A record or verification issue that requires review.
  • An identity or demographic information mismatch.
  • A technical or authentication-related issue.
  • A request that needs additional documents or clarification.

These are examples of issues to discuss with UIDAI, not confirmed reasons for every suspended Aadhaar case.

What should you do?

Contact UIDAI

Call the official helpline 1947 or use the official UIDAI website to ask about your exact Aadhaar status.

Ask for the exact reason

Explain the error message you received and ask which documents or process are required in your case.

Keep your reference number

Keep your complaint number, SRN, URN or EID safely for future follow-up. Never share OTPs or passwords.

Visit an authorised Aadhaar centre if advised

Use the official centre locator and follow the instructions given by UIDAI. Carry only the documents requested through official channels.

Follow up on your complaint

Check your complaint or request status through the official UIDAI portal. There is no guaranteed restoration time for every case.

Other Aadhaar Problems

Basic guidance for common application and update issues.

1. Aadhaar Authentication Problem

If fingerprint, iris or OTP authentication fails, check that the details entered are correct and that the relevant device or service is working. Ask the service provider for the exact error code. If the issue continues, contact UIDAI through official channels.

2. New Application Rejected as Duplicate

Aadhaar is designed as a unique identity number. If an application is rejected as a duplicate, do not keep submitting repeated applications. Contact UIDAI, share the official enrolment or rejection reference through the authorised channel and ask how your existing record can be reviewed.

3. Name or Document Mismatch

If your name or other details differ between documents, ask UIDAI which valid supporting documents are accepted for the specific update. Check spelling, order of names and document validity before submitting an update request.

4. Aadhaar Update Rejected

Check the rejection reason and whether the submitted document meets UIDAI requirements. If you do not understand the reason, contact UIDAI or visit an authorised Aadhaar centre. Do not rely on unofficial agents promising guaranteed approval.
