About Aadhaar Sevaa

Aadhaar Sevaa is an independent informational website created to help visitors find general guidance and official UIDAI resources.

Privacy & Safety

This website does not ask visitors to submit Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, passwords or identity documents. Use official UIDAI services for personal Aadhaar-related work.

Disclaimer

This is an independent help and information website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by UIDAI or the Government of India. Official services are provided only through official government websites.
