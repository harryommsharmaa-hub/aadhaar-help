Suspended / Deactivated Aadhaar

Understand the issue and follow official channels for help.

🔎

What does it mean?

A message saying suspended or deactivated needs to be checked with UIDAI. Do not assume the exact reason from one error message.

📋

Check your case

Review any official communication and contact UIDAI to confirm the actual status and required action.

☎️

Get official support

Call UIDAI helpline 1947 or use the official UIDAI website for guidance.

Remember: Aadhaar Sevaa cannot check, activate, restore or change anyone's Aadhaar. Do not share your Aadhaar number, OTP or documents on this website.

About Aadhaar Sevaa

Aadhaar Sevaa is an independent information website created to help people understand common Aadhaar-related issues and find official support resources.

Privacy Policy

This website does not ask visitors to submit Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, passwords or identity documents. Do not share sensitive personal information through public forms or messages. External official websites have their own privacy policies.

Disclaimer

Aadhaar Sevaa is an independent website and is not affiliated with, authorised by, or operated by UIDAI or the Government of India. Information is general guidance and is not a substitute for official confirmation. Rules and services may change; always verify with UIDAI.
