Aadhaar Problems & Solutions

Understand common Aadhaar problems and find official support.

Suspended or Deactivated Aadhaar

If your Aadhaar is suspended, deactivated or authentication is failing, first contact UIDAI through its official support channels.

The reason for the issue may vary. Only UIDAI can confirm the status and explain the appropriate resolution process.

Official UIDAI Helpline: 1947

What is SRN?

SRN means Service Request Number. It is a reference number associated with certain Aadhaar service requests.

What is URN?

URN means Update Request Number. It may be provided when an Aadhaar update request is submitted.

What is EID?

EID means Enrolment ID. It is used to identify an Aadhaar enrolment request.

How to Resolve Aadhaar Problems?

  1. Check your request status using the official UIDAI portal.
  2. Keep your acknowledgement or reference number available.
  3. Contact UIDAI helpline 1947 if the issue remains unresolved.
  4. Visit an authorised Aadhaar centre if UIDAI advises you to do so.
Important Security Notice: Never share your Aadhaar number, OTP, password or personal documents on this independent website. Use only official UIDAI services for sensitive activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common Aadhaar questions.

How can I check my Aadhaar status?

Visit the official UIDAI MyAadhaar portal and select the appropriate status service.

What should I do if my Aadhaar is suspended?

Contact UIDAI at 1947 or visit an authorised Aadhaar centre to understand the reason and next steps.

How can I check grievance status?

Use the official UIDAI grievance status facility with the required reference details.

How can I download e-Aadhaar?

Use the official UIDAI MyAadhaar portal to access e-Aadhaar download services.

What is the UIDAI helpline number?

The official UIDAI toll-free helpline number is 1947.

Where can I find an Aadhaar centre?

Use UIDAI's official centre locator to find nearby enrolment and update centres.

Can this website check my Aadhaar status?

No. This website provides information and links to official UIDAI services. Actual status checking takes place on UIDAI's portal.
