Aadhaar Problems & Solutions

Understand common Aadhaar problems and find official support.

Suspended or Deactivated Aadhaar

If your Aadhaar is suspended, deactivated or authentication is failing, contact UIDAI through its official support channels.

Only UIDAI can confirm the status and explain the appropriate resolution process.

Official UIDAI Helpline: 1947

What is SRN?

SRN means Service Request Number. It is a reference number associated with certain service requests.

What is URN?

URN means Update Request Number. It may be provided when an update request is submitted.

What is EID?

EID means Enrolment ID. It identifies an Aadhaar enrolment request.

How to Resolve Aadhaar Problems?

  1. Check your request status using the official UIDAI portal.
  2. Keep your acknowledgement or reference number available.
  3. Contact UIDAI helpline 1947 if the issue remains unresolved.
  4. Visit an authorised Aadhaar centre if UIDAI advises you.
Important Security Notice: Never share your Aadhaar number, OTP, password or personal documents on this website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my Aadhaar status?

Visit the official UIDAI portal and select the appropriate status service.

What should I do if my Aadhaar is suspended?

Contact UIDAI at 1947 or visit an authorised centre.

How can I check grievance status?

Use the official UIDAI grievance status facility.

How can I download e-Aadhaar?

Use the official UIDAI MyAadhaar portal.

What is the UIDAI helpline number?

The official UIDAI toll-free helpline is 1947.

Where can I find an Aadhaar centre?

Use UIDAI's official centre locator.

Can this website check my Aadhaar status?

No. Actual status checking takes place on UIDAI's official portal.
